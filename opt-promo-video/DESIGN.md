## Style Prompt

Vertical 9:16 OPT promotional video in a realistic commercial-ad direction. The visual world should feel like modern American campuses, offices, remote workspaces, project meetings, code/data work, career coaching, and compliance-aware employment workflows. The protagonist is a consistent Asian international student in simple business casual clothing. The first half should feel cool, quiet, and slightly anxious; after the OPT explanation and employer/project sections, the palette becomes brighter, warmer, and more hopeful.

## Colors

- Early background: `#101a24`, deep cool navy for uncertainty.
- Mid background: `#17242d`, professional dark blue-gray for explanation and structure.
- Late background: `#eef3ee`, warm light gray-green for clarity and progress.
- Primary foreground: `#f6f0e7` on dark scenes, `#1f2b30` on light scenes.
- Accent: `#7aa6bf` for planning, time, and data.
- Warm accent: `#d8aa67` for progress, guidance, and final action.
- Alert accent: `#c9584a` only for "not hang-name / not fake record" contrast.

## Typography

- Headlines: `"Newsreader", "Songti SC", serif` for a serious editorial voice with Chinese fallback.
- Body and subtitles: `"IBM Plex Sans", "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", sans-serif` for clean bilingual corporate readability.
- Numbers and timeline details: `"JetBrains Mono", "IBM Plex Mono", monospace` with tabular numbers.

## Motion

- Medium-energy commercial explainer pacing.
- Primary transition: blur crossfade/focus pull between related scenes.
- Accent transition: vertical push at topic changes.
- Scene entrances should be staggered and never pop in fully formed.
- No exit animation on scene content except the final CTA fade.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not imitate government websites, seals, forms, or endorsement screens.
- Do not imply guaranteed visa, identity, or job outcomes.
- Do not show identifiable pay stubs, IDs, student records, addresses, company secrets, or personal information.
- Do not create fake payroll or cash-transaction imagery.
- Do not use cartoon styling, distorted faces/hands, platform logos, watermarks, or unreadable generated text inside source footage.
